Apple Music Duplicate Finder v1.6
Requires Chrome or Edge 111 or newer.

INSTALL
1. Extract this ZIP into a folder you'll keep.
2. Open chrome://extensions (or edge://extensions) and turn on Developer mode.
3. Click Load unpacked and select this folder.

USE
1. Open a playlist on https://music.apple.com while signed in.
2. Click the extension icon and press Scan for duplicates.
3. Keep the separate scan window open until the scan finishes. You can click back into Apple Music while it runs.
4. Choose Start cleanup, remove copies in Apple Music, and mark your progress.
5. Choose Verify changes to run a fresh scan. Checkmarks alone do not confirm removal.

UPDATE
Extract the new ZIP over this folder and click Reload on the extensions page.

The extension only reads your playlist. It never changes your music, and
nothing leaves your browser.
